Fixture Launch Page

Today we are introducing the Fixture model, our most capable system so far for turning long articles into short vertical videos. It reads a page, keeps the numbers honest, and produces a storyboard that a human can review in under a minute. This fixture page exists only to exercise the extractor: it contains every media pattern the script must recognise, plus a comparison table and enough prose that a boilerplate-removal library treats it as an article rather than a navigation shell.

The model improves on its predecessor across every evaluation we ran. On the computer-use benchmark it reaches 72.6%, up from 65.7% for the previous generation, and it now finishes 9 of 10 spreadsheet tasks without asking for help. Latency dropped to 1.8 seconds for a first token, and the price is 30% lower than before. These numbers are the ones a downstream number checker must find in article.md, so they live in normal paragraphs as well as in the table below.

More patterns

Things that must be ignored: a duplicate of the hero image, a tracking pixel, an SVG logo and an inline data URI.

The closing paragraph exists to push the article length comfortably past the threshold at which the extractor trusts the boilerplate-removal output instead of falling back to the regex stripper. It also mentions that the rival scores in the table are vendor-reported values, which is exactly the kind of footnote a script writer needs to keep on screen.
